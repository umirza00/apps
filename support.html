Support

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email mirzaumt@gmail.com. Please include the app name, your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play a game?

Every game shows a “How to Play” screen the first time you open it, and you can open it again from the game’s menu at any time.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes this data, and it cannot be recovered.

Can I turn off sound or haptics?

Yes. Open the settings in each game’s menu. The apps also respect your device’s silent switch.

Do the apps collect any data or show ads?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
