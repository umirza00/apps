Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This privacy policy applies to our games (“the Apps”): Hush: 5 Relaxing Games, which includes five relaxing games (Zen Garden, Weave, Stars, My Garden and Light), and Nova Defense: Merge TD, a space strategy game. The same terms apply to both.

Summary

The Apps do not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect any personal information.

Data stored on your device

The Apps save your game progress and preferences (such as settings, saved gardens and canvases, puzzle progress, high scores and collected items) locally on your device only, using Apple’s standard on-device storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Removing an app deletes its data.

Motion data (Stars only)

The Stars game in Hush may read device-motion (accelerometer/gyroscope) readings to create a gentle parallax effect in the night sky. This data is used only in real time on your device and is never stored, logged or transmitted.

Children’s privacy

The Apps are suitable for all ages, collect no data, and contain no ads or external links.

Third parties

The Apps use no third-party services, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
